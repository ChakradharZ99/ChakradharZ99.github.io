Privacy Policy

Anime Shorts Studio. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Anime Shorts Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data it uses

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, Google provides an access token that lets the tool upload videos and read basic channel information (channel name, video count). The token is stored privately and used only to upload the owner's videos.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.
  • It does not use YouTube data for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
